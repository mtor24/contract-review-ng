LexReview NG: data-flow diagram

What happens to a contract, step by step, and what each step produces. All of it happens on the lawyer's own computer.
The lawyer does this The system does this automatically
What happens
What it produces
1
Upload a contractDrag a PDF, Word or text file onto the Review page.
The file, read on this computer only
2
Read and clean the textTakes the words out of the file and tidies quotes, page numbers and broken lines. ingest.py
Plain text of the whole contract
3
Confirm the contract typeThe system suggests a type from keywords; the lawyer keeps it or changes it. missing.py
Contract type, for example Lease or Loan
4
Split into clausesFinds where each numbered clause or heading starts and ends. segment.py
Clauses, each with its exact position in the text
5
Label each clauseMatches headings and phrases from the editable rules to name each clause, such as Termination or Governing Law. classify.py, clause_patterns.yaml
A clause type for every clause, and the words that produced it
6
Run the checksMissing clauses, risky wording, Nigerian compliance points, and obligations with their deadlines. missing.py, risk.py, compliance.py, obligations.py
Findings, each with a severity, a plain-English reason and the sentence it came from; deadlines as calendar dates where the contract gives one
7
SummarisePicks out the parties, dates, value, term and key sentences. summarise.py
Summary of the contract
8
Save to the local databaseStores the contract, the version, every clause and every finding, and indexes the text for search. storage/models.py
Stored records; every finding starts as a "Suggestion"
9
Review each findingChecks the source sentence, then accepts, rejects or edits the finding and adds a note.
The lawyer's decisions and notes, saved with each finding
10
Produce the outputsBuilds the report and keeps the register, deadlines, search and version comparison up to date. report.py, diff.py
PDF and Word report with the decisions, deadline list, search results, version comparison
