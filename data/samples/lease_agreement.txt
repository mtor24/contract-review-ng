LEASE AGREEMENT

This Agreement is made on the 5th day of September 2026.

BETWEEN:

(1) CRESCENT VIEW PROPERTIES LIMITED, a company incorporated in Nigeria with RC Number 1488207, having its registered office at 11 Adeola Odeku Street, Victoria Island, Lagos (hereinafter referred to as "the Landlord"); and

(2) HARBOURLINE LOGISTICS LIMITED, a company incorporated in Nigeria with RC Number 1755432, having its registered office at 42 Broad Street, Lagos Island, Lagos (hereinafter referred to as "the Tenant").

WHEREAS the Landlord is the owner of the commercial premises described herein and has agreed to let the same to the Tenant upon the terms and conditions contained in this Agreement.

PARTIES

The parties to this Agreement are the Landlord and the Tenant as described above, and this Agreement shall bind their successors in title and permitted assigns.

DEMISE

The Landlord hereby lets and the Tenant hereby takes the commercial premises known as Suites 4 to 7, Second Floor, Crescent View Plaza, 11 Adeola Odeku Street, Victoria Island, Lagos (hereinafter referred to as "the Premises"), together with the fixtures and fittings therein.

TERM

The tenancy created by this Agreement shall be for a term of three (3) years certain commencing on 1 October 2026 and expiring on 30 September 2029.

The Tenant shall have no right to hold over the Premises after the expiry of the term.

RENT

The Tenant shall pay to the Landlord an annual rent of N18,000,000 (Eighteen Million Naira) per annum, payable in advance on or before the first day of each rental year.

The first year's rent shall be paid in full within 14 days of the execution of this Agreement.

The Tenant shall also pay a service charge of N2,400,000 (Two Million Four Hundred Thousand Naira) per annum, payable in advance on the same dates as the rent.

RENT REVIEW

The rent shall be reviewed every two (2) years during the term and any renewal thereof.

Upon each review, the rent shall be increased by 25% over the rent payable immediately before the review date.

The Landlord shall serve a rent review notice on the Tenant at least 60 days before the relevant review date.

TENANT'S COVENANTS

The Tenant agrees to pay the rent and service charge on the dates and in the manner herein provided.

The Tenant shall use the Premises solely for the purposes of general business offices and logistics administration.

The Tenant must keep the interior of the Premises in good and tenantable repair and condition throughout the term.

The Tenant shall not make any structural alteration to the Premises without the prior written consent of the Landlord.

The Tenant will permit the Landlord or its agents to enter and inspect the Premises upon giving at least 48 hours prior written notice.

LANDLORD'S COVENANTS

The Landlord shall keep the structural parts of the building, including the roof, main walls and common areas, in good repair.

The Landlord agrees to insure the building against fire and allied perils to its full reinstatement value.

The Landlord shall provide water, security and waste disposal services to the common areas of the building.

REPAIRS

All repairs of a structural nature shall be the responsibility of the Landlord.

All repairs of a non-structural nature within the Premises shall be carried out by the Tenant at the Tenant's own cost within 30 days of written notice from the Landlord.

HOLDING OVER

If the Tenant holds over or remains in possession of the Premises after the expiry or sooner determination of the term, the Tenant shall pay to the Landlord a penalty of N500,000 for each day the Tenant so remains in possession, in addition to any other remedy available to the Landlord.

TERMINATION

The Landlord may terminate this Agreement if the rent or any part thereof remains unpaid for twenty-one (21) days after becoming due, or if the Tenant commits a material breach of any covenant herein and fails to remedy it within 30 days of written notice.

The Tenant may terminate this Agreement at the end of the second year of the term by giving the Landlord not less than six (6) months prior written notice.

Upon termination, the Tenant must yield up the Premises in good repair and condition within 7 days of the effective date of termination.

NOTICES

Any notice required or permitted under this Agreement shall be in writing and shall be deemed duly served if delivered by hand or sent by registered post or courier to the addresses of the parties stated above, or to such other address as a party may notify in writing.

A notice sent by registered post shall be deemed served within 7 days of posting; a notice delivered by hand shall be deemed served on the date of delivery.

GOVERNING LAW

This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the Federal Republic of Nigeria.

DISPUTE RESOLUTION

Any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Agreement shall be referred to arbitration by a sole arbitrator appointed by agreement of the parties, and the seat of the arbitration shall be Lagos.

The arbitration shall be conducted in accordance with the Arbitration and Mediation Act 2023.
