LOAN AGREEMENT

This Agreement is made on the 2nd day of November 2026.

BETWEEN:

(1) STERLING HARBOUR FINANCE LIMITED, a company incorporated in Nigeria with RC Number 1598430, having its registered office at 8 Kingsway Road, Ikoyi, Lagos (hereinafter referred to as "the Lender"); and

(2) CROWNPOINT PACKAGING INDUSTRIES LIMITED, a company incorporated in Nigeria with RC Number 1765102, having its registered office at 23 Oba Akran Avenue, Ikeja, Lagos (hereinafter referred to as "the Borrower").

WHEREAS the Lender has agreed to advance to the Borrower a term loan facility upon the terms and subject to the conditions contained in this Agreement.

NOW IT IS HEREBY AGREED as follows:

Clause 1: THE FACILITY

1.1 The Lender agrees to advance to the Borrower a term loan in the principal sum of N250,000,000 (Two Hundred and Fifty Million Naira) (hereinafter referred to as "the Loan").

1.2 The Loan shall be drawn in a single disbursement on 15 November 2026 (hereinafter referred to as "the Drawdown Date").

1.3 The Borrower shall apply the proceeds of the Loan exclusively towards the expansion of its packaging plant at 23 Oba Akran Avenue, Ikeja, Lagos.

Clause 2: INTEREST

2.1 The Loan shall bear interest at the rate of 28% per annum, calculated on the outstanding principal balance and payable monthly in arrears.

2.2 Accrued interest shall be paid together with each instalment of principal on the dates set out in the repayment schedule.

Clause 3: REPAYMENT

3.1 The Borrower shall repay the Loan, together with accrued interest, in 24 equal monthly instalments.

3.2 The first instalment shall fall due within 30 days of the Drawdown Date, and subsequent instalments shall fall due on the corresponding day of each succeeding month thereafter.

3.3 The Borrower must ensure that each instalment is received in the Lender's designated account within 3 days of its due date.

3.4 The Borrower may prepay the Loan in whole, but not in part, subject to giving the Lender at least 30 days prior written notice and paying a prepayment fee of 2% of the amount prepaid.

Clause 4: DEFAULT INTEREST

4.1 If the Borrower fails to pay any sum due under this Agreement on its due date, the unpaid sum shall attract default interest at the rate of 10% per month from the due date until the date of actual payment.

4.2 Default interest shall accrue daily and shall be compounded monthly.

Clause 5: SECURITY

5.1 The repayment of the Loan and the performance of the Borrower's obligations under this Agreement shall be secured by:

5.2 A legal mortgage over the Borrower's property situate at 23 Oba Akran Avenue, Ikeja, Lagos; and

5.3 A personal guarantee of the entire Loan given by Mr. Adewale Johnson Oyetunji, a director of the Borrower, of 5 Oduduwa Crescent, Ikeja GRA, Lagos.

5.4 The Borrower agrees to execute and deliver the security documents within 14 days of the date of this Agreement.

Clause 6: REPRESENTATIONS AND WARRANTIES

6.1 The Borrower represents and warrants that:

6.2 It is duly incorporated and validly existing under the laws of the Federal Republic of Nigeria.

6.3 It has full power and authority to enter into and perform this Agreement.

6.4 No event of default is subsisting, and the execution of this Agreement will not violate any agreement binding on the Borrower.

Clause 7: COVENANTS

7.1 The Borrower shall maintain comprehensive insurance over the mortgaged property to its full reinstatement value throughout the subsistence of the Loan.

7.2 The Borrower must deliver its audited financial statements to the Lender within 120 days of the end of each financial year.

7.3 The Borrower shall not incur any additional indebtedness exceeding N50,000,000 without the prior written consent of the Lender.

7.4 The Borrower will permit the Lender's representatives to inspect the mortgaged property and the Borrower's books within 7 days of a written request.

Clause 8: EVENTS OF DEFAULT

8.1 Each of the following shall constitute an event of default:

8.2 Failure by the Borrower to pay any instalment or other sum due under this Agreement within 7 days of its due date.

8.3 Any representation or warranty made by the Borrower proving to have been false or misleading in any material respect.

8.4 The Borrower becoming insolvent, entering into liquidation or having a receiver appointed over any of its assets.

8.5 Breach of any covenant in this Agreement which is not remedied within 14 days of written notice from the Lender.

Clause 9: ACCELERATION AND TERMINATION

9.1 Upon the occurrence of an event of default, the Lender may terminate this Agreement and demand immediate repayment without notice.

9.2 Upon such demand, the entire outstanding principal, accrued interest and default interest shall become immediately due and payable.

9.3 The Lender may thereafter enforce the security described in Clause 5.

Clause 10: GENERAL

10.1 No failure or delay by the Lender in exercising any right shall operate as a waiver of that right.

10.2 This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties in relation to its subject matter.

10.3 The Borrower may not assign its rights or obligations under this Agreement. The Lender may assign its rights to any affiliate or financial institution.

Clause 11: GOVERNING LAW AND JURISDICTION

11.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the Federal Republic of Nigeria.

11.2 The parties hereby submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of the High Court of Lagos State in respect of any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.
