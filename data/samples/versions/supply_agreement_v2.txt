SUPPLY AGREEMENT

This Agreement is made on the 20th day of October 2026.

BETWEEN:

(1) GOLDEN ROOT AGRO-PROCESSORS LIMITED, a company incorporated in Nigeria with RC Number 1827401, having its principal place of business at Km 12 Lagos-Ibadan Expressway, Mowe, Ogun State (hereinafter referred to as "the Supplier"); and

(2) MAINLAND FOODS DISTRIBUTION PLC, a company incorporated in Nigeria with RC Number 1693455, having its registered office at 17 Apapa-Oshodi Expressway, Isolo, Lagos (hereinafter referred to as "the Buyer").

WHEREAS the Supplier carries on the business of processing and packaging cassava flour and the Buyer desires to purchase such products from the Supplier on the terms of this Agreement.

NOW IT IS HEREBY AGREED as follows:

Clause 1: DEFINITIONS

1.1 "Products" means processed food-grade cassava flour packaged in 50kg bags conforming to the specifications in Schedule 1.

1.2 "Contract Value" means the total value of N120,000,000 (One Hundred and Twenty Million Naira) for the initial term.

1.3 "Delivery Point" means the Buyer's warehouse at 17 Apapa-Oshodi Expressway, Isolo, Lagos.

Clause 2: SUPPLY OF PRODUCTS

2.1 The Supplier shall supply and deliver to the Buyer a minimum of forty (40) metric tonnes of the Products per calendar month during the term.

2.2 The Supplier shall deliver each consignment of the Products to the Delivery Point within 14 days of receipt of the Buyer's purchase order.

2.3 The Supplier must ensure that every consignment is accompanied by a quality certificate within 7 days of dispatch from the Supplier's premises.

2.4 The Buyer agrees to inspect each consignment and notify the Supplier of any defect within 10 days of delivery.

Clause 3: PRICE AND PAYMENT

3.1 The total contract value for the initial term is N120,000,000 (One Hundred and Twenty Million Naira).

3.2 The Buyer shall pay each invoice submitted by the Supplier within 30 days of invoice.

3.3 Late payments shall attract interest at the rate of 1.5% per month.

3.4 The Supplier will issue invoices monthly in arrears, and each invoice shall state the quantity delivered and the applicable unit price.

Clause 4: LIABILITY

4.1 The Supplier's total liability under this Agreement shall not exceed the Contract Value.

4.2 The Buyer shall not in any circumstances be liable for any loss of profit, loss of revenue or indirect loss suffered by the Supplier.

4.3 The Supplier agrees to bear all losses arising from defective Products, delayed delivery or non-delivery.

Clause 5: INDEMNITY

5.1 The Supplier shall indemnify and hold harmless the Buyer against all claims, losses, damages, costs and expenses arising out of or in connection with the supply of the Products, any defect in the Products, or any breach of this Agreement by the Supplier.

5.2 The indemnity in Clause 5.1 shall survive the termination or expiry of this Agreement.

Clause 6: TERM AND RENEWAL

6.1 This Agreement shall commence on 1 November 2026 and shall continue for an initial term of one (1) year.

6.2 This Agreement shall automatically renew for successive periods of one (1) year.

6.3 The terms applicable during each renewal period shall be the same as the terms of the initial term unless otherwise agreed in writing.

Clause 7: FORCE MAJEURE

7.1 Neither party shall be liable for any failure to perform its obligations under this Agreement where such failure results from events beyond its reasonable control, including flood, fire, epidemic, war, riot, strike or government action.

7.2 The affected party shall notify the other party of the force majeure event within 7 days of its occurrence.

7.3 If the force majeure event continues for more than ninety (90) days, either party may terminate this Agreement by written notice.

Clause 8: GOVERNING LAW

8.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the Federal Republic of Nigeria.

Clause 9: DISPUTE RESOLUTION

9.1 Any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Agreement shall be referred to and finally resolved by arbitration under the Arbitration and Mediation Act 2023.

9.2 The seat of the arbitration shall be Lagos, Nigeria, and the tribunal shall consist of a sole arbitrator.

9.3 The language of the arbitration shall be English.

Clause 10: NOTICES

10.1 Any notice under this Agreement shall be in writing and delivered by hand, by courier or by email to the addresses stated in this Agreement.

10.2 A notice is deemed received on delivery, if delivered by hand or courier, or on the date of transmission, if sent by email before 5pm on a business day.

Clause 11: GENERAL

11.1 This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties in relation to its subject matter.

11.2 No amendment of this Agreement shall be effective unless in writing and signed by both parties.

11.3 The Buyer may set off any amount owed to it by the Supplier against any amount payable to the Supplier under this Agreement.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF the parties have executed this Agreement the day and year first above written.
