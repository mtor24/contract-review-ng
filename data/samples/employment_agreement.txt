EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT

This Agreement is made on the 14th day of November 2026.

BETWEEN:

(1) ZENITH CREST INDUSTRIES LIMITED, a company incorporated under the laws of the Federal Republic of Nigeria with RC Number 1748823, having its registered office at 24 Adetokunbo Ademola Street, Victoria Island, Lagos (hereinafter referred to as "the Employer"); and

(2) MR. CHUKWUEMEKA ADEWALE OKAFOR of 15 Bourdillon Road, Ikoyi, Lagos (hereinafter referred to as "the Employee").

WHEREAS:

A. The Employer is engaged in the business of manufacturing and distribution of consumer goods within Nigeria.
B. The Employer desires to employ the Employee as Regional Operations Manager, and the Employee has agreed to serve the Employer on the terms and conditions contained in this Agreement.

NOW IT IS HEREBY AGREED as follows:

1. APPOINTMENT AND COMMENCEMENT

1.1 The Employer hereby employs the Employee and the Employee agrees to serve the Employer as Regional Operations Manager.

1.2 The employment under this Agreement shall commence on 1 December 2026 and shall continue unless and until terminated in accordance with the provisions of this Agreement.

1.3 The Employee shall be on probation for a period of six (6) months from the commencement date.

2. DUTIES AND RESPONSIBILITIES

2.1 The Employee shall perform such duties as are customarily associated with the position of Regional Operations Manager and such other duties as the Employer may reasonably assign.

2.2 The Employee shall devote the whole of his time, attention and abilities to the business of the Employer during normal working hours.

2.3 The Employee agrees to comply with all lawful and reasonable directions of the board of directors of the Employer.

2.4 The Employee must submit a written operations report to the Managing Director within 7 days of the end of each calendar month.

3. SALARY AND BENEFITS

3.1 The Employer shall pay the Employee a monthly salary of N850,000 (Eight Hundred and Fifty Thousand Naira), payable in arrears on the last working day of each month.

3.2 The Employer will provide the Employee with a motor vehicle for official use within 30 days of the commencement date.

3.3 The Employee shall be entitled to twenty-one (21) working days of paid annual leave in each calendar year.

3.4 The Employer shall enroll the Employee in the Employer's group health insurance scheme within 14 days of the commencement date.

4. CONFIDENTIALITY

4.1 The Employee shall not, either during the employment or at any time after its termination, disclose to any person any confidential information relating to the business, customers, suppliers, trade secrets or affairs of the Employer.

4.2 All documents, records, price lists and customer databases made or acquired by the Employee in the course of employment shall be and remain the property of the Employer.

4.3 Upon termination of employment, the Employee must return all confidential materials to the Employer within 7 days of the termination date.

5. NON-COMPETE

5.1 The Employee shall not, for a period of 24 months following the termination of his employment, directly or indirectly engage in or be concerned with any business which competes with the business of the Employer within the Federal Republic of Nigeria.

5.2 The Employee shall not, for a period of 24 months following termination, solicit or entice away any customer, client or employee of the Employer.

5.3 The Employee agrees that the restrictions in this clause are reasonable and necessary for the protection of the Employer's legitimate business interests.

6. TERMINATION

6.1 The Employer may terminate this Agreement at any time without notice.

6.2 The Employee may terminate this Agreement by giving the Employer three (3) months' written notice.

6.3 Upon termination, the Employee must vacate any official accommodation and return all company property within 14 days of the effective date of termination.

6.4 The Employer shall pay the Employee any accrued but unpaid salary within 30 days of the effective date of termination.

7. PERSONAL DATA

7.1 The Employee acknowledges that the Employer will process the Employee's personal data, including contact details, bank account information and next of kin details, for the purposes of administering the employment relationship.

7.2 The Employee consents to the processing described in clause 7.1.

8. GENERAL

8.1 This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties and supersedes all prior arrangements and understandings.

8.2 No variation of this Agreement shall be valid unless made in writing and signed by both parties.

8.3 If any provision of this Agreement is held to be invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall continue in full force and effect.

8.4 The Employer may assign this Agreement to any successor in title to its business.

9. GOVERNING LAW

9.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the Federal Republic of Nigeria.
