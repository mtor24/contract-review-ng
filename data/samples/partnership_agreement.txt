PARTNERSHIP AGREEMENT

This Agreement is made on the 8th day of November 2026.

BETWEEN:

(1) MRS. ADAEZE CHIDINMA OKORO of 14 Mississippi Street, Maitama, Abuja (hereinafter referred to as "the First Partner");

(2) MR. BABATUNDE OLUSEGUN AKINOLA of 22 Gimbiya Street, Area 11, Garki, Abuja (hereinafter referred to as "the Second Partner"); and

(3) MRS. YETUNDE OMOLARA FASHOLA of 6 Katsina Ala Street, Maitama, Abuja (hereinafter referred to as "the Third Partner").

WHEREAS the parties have agreed to carry on business in partnership as management consultants under the terms and conditions set out in this Agreement.

NOW IT IS HEREBY AGREED as follows:

1. FORMATION AND NAME

1.1 The parties hereby constitute themselves partners in a partnership firm carrying on business under the name "MERIDIAN ADVISORY PARTNERS" (hereinafter referred to as "the Firm").

1.2 The principal place of business of the Firm shall be at 18 Nile Street, Maitama, Abuja, or such other place as the partners may agree.

1.3 The partnership shall commence on 1 December 2026 and shall continue unless dissolved in accordance with this Agreement.

2. NATURE OF BUSINESS

2.1 The business of the Firm shall be the provision of management consulting, business advisory and corporate training services.

2.2 The Firm shall not engage in any other business without the unanimous written consent of all partners.

3. CAPITAL CONTRIBUTIONS

3.1 The initial capital of the Firm shall be N45,000,000 (Forty-Five Million Naira), contributed by the partners as follows:

3.2 The First Partner shall contribute N20,000,000 (Twenty Million Naira).

3.3 The Second Partner shall contribute N15,000,000 (Fifteen Million Naira).

3.4 The Third Partner shall contribute N10,000,000 (Ten Million Naira).

3.5 Each partner must pay his or her capital contribution into the Firm's bank account within 30 days of the commencement date.

3.6 No partner shall be entitled to interest on capital contributed.

4. PROFIT SHARING

4.1 The net profits and losses of the Firm shall be shared among the partners in the following ratio:

4.2 The First Partner: 45% (forty-five per cent).

4.3 The Second Partner: 33% (thirty-three per cent).

4.4 The Third Partner: 22% (twenty-two per cent).

4.5 Profits shall be distributed within 60 days of the adoption of the Firm's annual accounts.

5. MANAGEMENT

5.1 The First Partner shall serve as the Managing Partner and shall be responsible for the day-to-day administration of the Firm.

5.2 Each partner shall devote such time and attention to the business of the Firm as is reasonably necessary for its proper conduct.

5.3 The following decisions shall require the unanimous consent of all partners:

5.4 (i) borrowing any sum exceeding N5,000,000; (ii) admitting a new partner; (iii) disposing of any substantial asset of the Firm; (iv) changing the nature of the Firm's business.

5.5 The partners shall hold a partners' meeting at least once every calendar quarter, and minutes of each meeting shall be recorded within 7 days of the meeting.

6. BANKING AND ACCOUNTS

6.1 The Firm shall maintain a bank account with a reputable Nigerian bank, and all cheques above N2,000,000 shall require the signatures of any two partners.

6.2 Proper books of account shall be kept, and the accounts of the Firm shall be audited annually within 90 days of the end of each financial year.

7. ADMISSION OF NEW PARTNERS

7.1 No new partner shall be admitted to the Firm except with the unanimous written consent of all existing partners.

7.2 A new partner shall contribute such capital as may be agreed and shall execute a deed of adherence within 30 days of admission.

8. RETIREMENT AND EXPULSION

8.1 Any partner may retire from the Firm by giving not less than six (6) months' written notice to the other partners.

8.2 A partner may be expelled by a resolution passed by not less than two-thirds of the other partners where that partner has committed a material breach of this Agreement and failed to remedy it within 30 days of written notice.

8.3 The share of a retiring or expelled partner shall be paid out within 180 days of the effective date of retirement or expulsion.

9. DISSOLUTION AND TERMINATION

9.1 The partnership may be dissolved by the unanimous written agreement of all partners.

9.2 The partnership shall be dissolved automatically upon the death or bankruptcy of any partner, unless the remaining partners agree in writing to continue the business within 30 days of such event.

9.3 Upon dissolution, the assets of the Firm shall be realised and applied first in payment of the Firm's debts, and the balance distributed among the partners in proportion to their profit-sharing ratios.

9.4 The partners agree to wind up the affairs of the Firm in an orderly manner within 12 months of the date of dissolution.

10. RESTRAINT AFTER CEASING TO BE A PARTNER

10.1 A partner who ceases to be a partner shall not, for a period of twelve (12) months, carry on a competing consulting business within the Federal Capital Territory.

11. GOVERNING LAW

11.1 This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the Federal Republic of Nigeria.

12. DISPUTE RESOLUTION

12.1 Any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Agreement shall be referred to arbitration by a sole arbitrator agreed by the parties, and the seat of the arbitration shall be Abuja.

12.2 The decision of the arbitrator shall be final and binding on the partners.
